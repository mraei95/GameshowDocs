SECTION 03

3. Data We Explicitly Do NOT Collect

  • No Voice Recording: We never record, process, or store voice audio.
  • No Message Scraping: We do not log general chat messages or private DMs.
  • No Real-World Identifiers: No emails, phone numbers, real names, or physical addresses.
  • No Financial Info: We never see or store payment card details.
SECTION 04

4. Voice & Audio Privacy Guarantee

Guaranteed: Zero Voice Audio Capture Stage Host does NOT tap into, listen to, process, record, transcript, or transmit voice audio streams from any Discord voice or stage channel.

The Bot solely subscribes to Discord's GuildVoiceStates gateway event to observe metadata: specifically, whether a participant's voice state indicates they are currently a Speaker (suppress: false) or an Audience member (suppress: true).

SECTION 05

5. How We Use Collected Information

Information is used solely to execute the live stage game: counting votes locked in during the 15-second timer, computing correct answers, moving eliminated players down to the audience, and enabling the host revive menu.

SECTION 06

6. Data Storage & Retention Period

Votes and active round states are stored strictly in volatile system memory (RAM). When a game concludes or when the bot restarts, all round votes and transient game data are completely purged.

SECTION 07

7. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties. Data is exchanged only with Discord Inc. via official API endpoints and our hosting servers.

SECTION 08

8. User Rights & Data Erasure (GDPR / CCPA)

Users and server owners have the right to request deletion of any custom uploaded question packs or server settings. Kicking the Bot from your server immediately terminates all data processing for your guild.

SECTION 09

9. Security Safeguards

All communication with Discord is secured with TLS 1.3 and WSS encryption. Bot tokens and server credentials are strictly isolated in environment variables.

SECTION 10

For privacy inquiries, data deletion requests, or compliance reviews, please reach out via our GitHub repository or Discord community.
